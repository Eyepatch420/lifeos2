Privacy Policy — DigiDaily

Last updated: August 10, 2026
In short: DigiDaily is an offline, on-device personal utility app. It does not have a backend server, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not transmit, sell, or share your personal data with anyone. All data you enter stays on your device.

1. Who we are

DigiDaily is developed by Igurus ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the DigiDaily Android application handles information when you use it.

2. Data we collect

DigiDaily does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or with any third party. The app has no backend server and no account system.

Data you create — reminders, planner entries, expenses, health notes, membership records, bills, documents, and any photos or files you attach — is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We never see it.

3. Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsTo show reminders, planner alerts, and bill/membership due-date notifications you schedule.
Exact alarms / boot-completedSo scheduled reminders still fire at the correct time, including after your device restarts.
Biometric / device authenticationOptional app-lock feature to protect access to your data using your device's fingerprint/face unlock. Biometric data itself is handled entirely by the operating system and never reaches DigiDaily or us.
Camera / photo libraryTo let you attach photos to entries (e.g. receipts, documents) and to scan text from images using on-device text recognition. Images are processed and stored locally only.
File storage accessTo let you attach, export, or back up files and documents you choose within the app.

4. On-device text recognition

DigiDaily uses Google's ML Kit for on-device text recognition (e.g. scanning receipts or documents). This processing happens entirely on your device — images are not uploaded to any server for this feature.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, analytics providers, or any third party, because we do not collect it in the first place.

6. Data storage and backup

All app data is stored locally on your device. If you use the app's export or share feature, files are only sent to the destination you explicitly choose (e.g. your own cloud storage, email, or another app) — this action is initiated and controlled entirely by you.

7. Children's privacy

DigiDaily is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal data at all.

8. Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time from within the app, or by uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data from your device.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
